HACKATHON SEPTEMBER 25–26, 2026

Open Hackathon: Challenge #11 — LOST IN SPACE

An ADHD Lived Experience | Team MVPrompt

Challenge Origin: Grigoris Lemonis — Managing Director @ Human Intelligence

Team Name: MVPrompt

The Problem

Many people with ADHD struggle to stay focused, organize their day, manage time, and complete tasks. Small distractions can quickly derail attention, while constant mental overload and sensory stimuli increase stress, making it difficult to stay on track.

Existing productivity tools often fail because they require more organization than the user can consistently maintain.

The Challenge

"How might we help people with ADHD stay focused, organized, and in control of their daily lives without adding complexity or becoming another distraction?"

Our Solution

With team MVPrompt, we are building an adaptive AI companion designed to support focus without introducing friction or extra anxiety.

Instead of complex dashboards, the user simply shares what they want to work on and how they are currently feeling. The app dynamically adapts, offering gentle, well-timed reminders if focus drifts. The goal is to keep the experience simple, supportive, and deeply personalized — helping users return to interrupted tasks smoothly.

Key Angles We're Exploring:

  • Context-Aware Guidance: Smart, non-intrusive nudges when focus drops.
  • Adaptive Friction: Lowering administrative effort when mental fatigue is high.
  • Sensory-Friendly UX: Minimal visual noise and calm interaction loops.

Updates, code snippets, and key learnings will be posted here as we build during September 25–26!
